ENTREW is a public, backendless developer-program eligibility review platform. An organizer signs an immutable 2–3-criterion assessment rubric and deadline. An applicant signs an entry tied to a public GitHub repository, exact full commit SHA, bounded evidence files, and a program/wallet/repo claim marker inside that same revision. Product outcomes:
- all 2–3 mandatory criteria `MET` + valid required provenance => `QUALIFIED`;
- proven substantive mandatory criterion `NOT_MET` on sufficient bound evidence => `NOT_ESTABLISHED`;

The qualified roster MUST come only from contract state, and only after a genuine finalized successful execution and canonical readback. No organizer outcome override. Enforce one entry per wallet/program (not one human), maximum four revisions, no conclusive identical-snapshot replay, and at most two cooldown-governed inconclusive rechecks.
